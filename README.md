# PIMS Business Intelligence & Management Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a management and Business Intelligence analysis of a hospital information management environment. The project combines management concepts, data-driven decision-making, process analysis, strategic planning, risk management, IoMT considerations, and cybersecurity awareness.

The project demonstrates how technical and management approaches can be integrated to improve organizational operations, decision-making, efficiency, and security within a healthcare environment.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

* Analyze organizational and operational challenges within a hospital environment.
* Apply management and strategic analysis techniques.
* Examine existing business processes and identify areas for improvement.
* Develop improved TO-BE processes based on identified limitations.
* Apply Business Intelligence concepts for data-driven decision-making.
* Develop Power BI dashboards for analysis and visualization.
* Analyze organizational risks and potential mitigation strategies.
* Examine the role of IoMT in modern healthcare environments.
* Consider cybersecurity risks associated with connected healthcare systems.
* Integrate technical and management concepts into a practical case study.

---

## 🏥 Project Context

The project focuses on a hospital environment where information systems, operational processes, connected medical devices, and management decisions are interconnected.

The analysis considers how improved information management and Business Intelligence can support areas such as:

* Hospital operations
* Resource management
* Decision-making
* Process optimization
* Risk management
* Healthcare technology
* IoMT infrastructure
* Cybersecurity

## 🔄 Process Analysis

The project examines existing **AS-IS** processes and proposes improved **TO-BE** processes.

The purpose of this analysis is to identify:

* Existing process limitations
* Operational inefficiencies
* Areas for improvement
* Opportunities for automation
* Improved information flow
* Better decision-making processes

---

## 📈 Strategic Analysis

Several management and strategic analysis techniques were considered during the project, including:

* SWOT Analysis
* PESTEL Analysis
* Porter's Five Forces
* BCG Matrix
* Risk Analysis
* Process Analysis

These frameworks were used to understand the organizational environment and support strategic decision-making.

---

## 🔐 IoMT & Cybersecurity Considerations

Modern healthcare environments increasingly depend on connected medical devices and information systems.

The project therefore considers the relationship between **Internet of Medical Things (IoMT)** infrastructure and cybersecurity.

Potential security considerations include:

* Protection of connected medical devices
* Data confidentiality
* Data integrity
* System availability
* Network security
* Access control
* Risk management
* Security of connected healthcare infrastructure

This demonstrates the importance of considering cybersecurity alongside technological and management decisions in healthcare environments.
